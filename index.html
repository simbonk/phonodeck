BROWSER VOICE (FALLBACK)

Used when the Gemini voice is off, unavailable or out of quota. On iPhone and iPad the app lets Safari use its own default voice: web pages can only use Apple's basic built-in voices there (downloaded Enhanced or Premium voices are not available to Safari), so it sounds robotic. On a computer, Automatic picks the best voice (marked ★), such as Microsoft Ana Online (Natural) on Edge.

Hear nothing on iPhone or iPad? Silent Mode mutes the device voice and the background sounds (the Gemini voice still plays). To turn it off, swipe down from the top-right corner of the screen to open Control Center and tap the bell so it is no longer crossed out. Older iPads and iPhones may have a switch on the side instead; newer iPhones can also use the Action button.

WELCOME TO THE PHONODECK

SET UP IN ABOUT A MINUTE

  1. Get a Gemini API key at aistudio.google.com/apikey and set up billing for it (a small prepaid balance works).
  2. Paste it below.
  3. Press Start.

Your key is stored only in this browser and sent only to Google. Use a key with a low spending cap, and do not enter it on a shared computer.

BEFORE YOU START

  1. Use Chrome, Edge or Safari. Firefox can't turn speech into text, so hands-free talking won't work there.
  2. Turn your sound on. A narrator reads the story aloud, so turn the volume up. On an iPhone or iPad, also make sure Silent Mode is off (in Control Center, the bell should not be crossed out).
  3. Test the microphone. Allow it when your browser asks, then say a few words. If the words appear here, hands-free works: tap 🎧 Hands-free next to Send, talk, and say "make it so" to send.

This browser can't listen. Hands-free talking needs Chrome, Edge or Safari: Firefox can't turn speech into text. The phonodeck is at its best when you talk to it, so please open this page in one of those browsers. You can still type if you prefer.
